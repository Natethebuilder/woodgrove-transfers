# WGB-104 · Instant transfer fee

**Type:** Feature · **Priority:** Medium · **Component:** `src/transfer.js`, transfer console

## Story

> "Instant transfers arrive within 10 seconds, and from 1 January we charge a small fee for them. Standard transfers stay free. Customers must see the fee before they send the money."
>
> — Product owner, Payments

## Rules

- Instant transfers cost **0.1% of the amount**, rounded half up to whole cents.
- Minimum fee **€ 0,20**, maximum fee **€ 2,50**.
- Standard transfers are free (fee € 0,00).
- `createTransfer` adds a `feeCents` field to the transfer.
- The transfer console shows the fee.

## Examples

| Amount | Instant | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| € 100,00 | yes | € 0,20 (minimum) |
| € 1.234,56 | yes | € 1,23 |
| € 10.000,00 | yes | € 2,50 (maximum) |
| € 500,00 | no | € 0,00 |

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `feeCents` follows the rules and the examples above.
- [ ] Tests cover every example, plus the rounding (for example € 1.235,00 → € 1,24).
- [ ] The console shows the fee in the result panel.
- [ ] All tests pass.
